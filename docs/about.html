About TrendWatch Philippines

TrendWatch Philippines is an independent informational project that presents topics appearing in Google Trends for the Philippines, with explanatory pages generated through an automated publishing workflow.

What we cover

The project is intended to help readers identify topics receiving search interest and find related public news coverage. Trend data indicates search interest; it does not by itself establish the cause, accuracy, or importance of a topic.

Sources and editorial limitations

Trend topics and related headlines may come from the Google Trends RSS feed. Article drafts may be produced with an AI service from limited trend and headline context. Automated content can be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect, and a mention of a topic is not independent verification of its claims.

When available, related source links are provided so readers can consult the original publisher. Please verify important, time-sensitive, health, safety, financial, or legal information with authoritative sources before acting on it.

To avoid publishing a single-headline recap, the automated workflow requires linked reporting from at least two publisher domains before it drafts an article. This is a minimum evidence check, not independent verification; the outlets may rely on the same underlying report.

Existing articles should not be treated as independently fact-checked unless a page explicitly says so. New AI-generated articles are currently held from automated publication while a human editorial review process is established. An optional source-by-source AI comparison can help editors identify claims to check, but its results are advisory and do not replace reading the original sources.

Independence
