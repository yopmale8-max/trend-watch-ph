Protect your privacy

The issue tracker is public. Do not include passwords, private contact details, account identifiers, health information, or other sensitive or personal information. A private support or privacy-request email has not been configured for this project.

Corrections

When reporting a content issue, include the page URL and a brief description of the correction. Link to a reliable public source where possible, and avoid posting information that you do not have permission to share.
