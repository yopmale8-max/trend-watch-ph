Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This page describes the data practices reflected in the current TrendWatch Philippines website and publishing code. It is general information, not a legal determination. Hosting-provider practices may differ and are controlled by the provider.

Information handled by this website

The website does not currently provide visitor accounts, comments, contact forms, or a checkout service. The current site code does not intentionally store visitor preferences in browser storage.

The dashboard may request a trends data file from the same site if one is configured. The current dashboard can show illustrative sample data when live data is unavailable; sample values are not actual search statistics.

Automated content and third-party services

The publishing workflow can process publicly available Google Trends RSS data. When article generation is run, the trend title, feed publication date, related news headlines, publisher names, URLs, and snippets, plus up to 7,000 characters extracted from each of up to five linked source pages, may be sent to the Google Gemini API to draft content. Approximate search-volume labels are not included in the generation request. Do not submit private or sensitive personal information through this site. Processing by Google is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy disclosures.

New article candidates are staged with an AI-assisted, source-by-source comparison before publication. For that comparison, the candidate article text, linked source URLs and labels, and up to 7,000 characters of text extracted from each fetched source page may be sent to the Google Gemini API. The comparison is advisory, not fact-checking, and cannot publish an article. Candidate pages and the report are stored as a GitHub Actions artifact for up to seven days. Publication is blocked unless a required human reviewer approves the GitHub Actions environment. The source page's full HTML is not included in the report.

The site may link to external news articles and services. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Opening an external link may disclose information to that provider or your browser, according to their settings and policies.

Analytics and advertising

Pages load Cloudflare Web Analytics to collect aggregate website usage and performance information, such as page views and visits. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not track individual visitors. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for information about its processing.

The site does not currently configure advertising scripts. Before Google ads are enabled, this policy will be updated with the applicable disclosures about advertising cookies, personalized advertising, and user choices, and any legally required consent experience will be implemented. See Google's privacy-policy requirements for AdSense. These advertising practices are not active on this site today.

Hosting and security

The site is published as static files through an automated repository workflow. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, timestamps, or browser details, in server logs for delivery, security, and operations. Consult the actual hosting provider's privacy documentation for its handling and retention practices; those details are not configured in this repository.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can control cookies and site data through your browser settings. The current site code does not require browser storage for its basic pages. For privacy requests, contact the site by email using the address on the Contact page; do not use the public issue tracker for personal or sensitive information. Email is not a guarantee of secure or confidential delivery.

Changes to this policy

This policy should be revised when the site's data practices or service providers change. Continued use of the site after an update means you have seen the revised notice; it does not replace any consent required by applicable law.
