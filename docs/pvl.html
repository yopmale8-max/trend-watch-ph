Frequently Asked Questions

What does PVL stand for?

PVL stands for the Premier Volleyball League, the leading professional women's volleyball league in the Philippines organized by Sports Vision Management Group, Inc.

Why is the PVL currently trending in the Philippines?

The PVL trends regularly due to intense fan interest surrounding live matchdays, rivalries between top clubs, standout player performances, and conference playoff races.

Where can fans watch PVL games live?

Matches are broadcast live nationwide on free and cable TV via One Sports and One Sports+, and streamed digitally through the Pilipinas Live mobile app and PVL official platforms.
